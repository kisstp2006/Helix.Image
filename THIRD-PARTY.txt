  stb_image v2.30 by Sean Barrett, http://nothings.org/stb
  Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nothings/stb/master/stb_image.h (2026-11-10)
  SHA-256: 594c2fe35d49488b4382dbfaec8f98366defca819d916ac95becf3e75f4200b3
  Used for JPEG, BMP, TGA, PSD and GIF (Source/Helix/Image/Codecs/Implementation/StbDecoders.cpp).
  The file is unchanged. Known faults and how the wrapper avoids them:
  - stbi_is_16_bit does not find 16 bit PSD files outside MSVC: STBI_NOTUSED(v) is (void)sizeof(v) there, so the
    header fields it should skip are not read. The wrapper reads the PSD depth itself.
  - Damaged headers make it allocate huge blocks; the wrapper's allocator refuses blocks larger than any texture.
